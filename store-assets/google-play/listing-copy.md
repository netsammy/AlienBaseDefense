## Short Description

## Full Description

Alien Base Defense is a fast single-player 3D survival shooter where alien waves attack your futuristic defense zone.

Stay inside the shield perimeter, move quickly, aim carefully, and stop enemies before they break through the base core. Survive wave after wave, earn coins, upgrade your rifle and base shield, and push deeper through themed city battlegrounds.

Features:
- 3D alien wave survival gameplay
- Touch controls with 360 degree look
- Alien radar map
- Multiple stages inspired by city defense zones
- Rifle, base shield, fire rate, and turret upgrades
- Health and ammo pickups
- Optional rewarded ads to continue after defeat

No account is required. Progress is saved locally on your device.
