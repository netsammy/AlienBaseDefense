Alien Base Defense Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Overview

Alien Base Defense is a single-player game. The app does not require an account and does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information.

Local Game Data

The game stores progress data locally on your device, including best score, coins, selected stage, graphics setting, and permanent upgrade levels. This data is stored through browser local storage and remains on your device unless you clear the app or browser storage.

Network Use

The game is designed to run from its installed app files or hosted web app assets. It does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, account login, multiplayer, or cloud save features.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Support

For privacy or support questions, contact the publisher through the support information provided on the Microsoft Store listing.
